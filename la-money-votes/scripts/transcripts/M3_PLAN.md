## M3.2 — Coverage badge

- Add a read-only coverage RPC or equivalent aggregate contract.
- Render `N speaking turns indexed across M meetings` above the search input.
- Define a deterministic thin-coverage threshold from the expanded corpus
  distribution; do not hard-code the old 16-chunk minimum.
- Preserve the current rule that officials with no aliases do not show the
  transcript widget.

### Implementation and reconciliation

- `get_transcript_coverage` counts `sub_chunk_idx = 0` rows so split long turns
  contribute one speaking turn, and counts distinct `video_id` values for the
  meeting total. The embedding model is pinned to prevent double-counting during
  a future model migration.
- `/api/transcript-coverage` is read-only, cacheable, and returns counts plus a
  `thin` disclosure flag. It does not call the embedding API.
- The provisional thin rule is fewer than 20 speaking turns or fewer than 5
  meetings. This is derived from the current direct-attribution distribution
  (11–51 turns across 4–9 meetings), not the retired 16-chunk minimum. Recompute
  it after M3.1 expands the indexed corpus.
- The coverage line always states that the index is not a complete public
  record. Thin coverage adds that searches may miss relevant statements.
- Known attribution limitation: `cd8-official` currently has no direct count
  because CART identifies Marqueece Harris-Dawson as `Council President`, whose
  rows resolve to the procedural `council-president` ID. M3.2 reports the gap
  rather than introducing a name or keyword fallback; M3.4 must adjudicate it.
- Supabase migration `20260820042036_m3_transcript_coverage` is applied. All 15
  roster entries reconcile exactly between direct indexed-row aggregation and
  the RPC for turns, meetings, and first/last dates; CD8 reconciles at 0/0.
- The indexed model/version remains one cohort:
  `pplx-embed-v1-0.6b` version 1, 5,003 chunk rows, 3,341 parent-turn rows,

## Key risks

- PrimeGov contains SAP duplicates, no-video rows, and multiple meeting records
  that can share one YouTube video.
- Caption-track availability cannot be inferred from PrimeGov's video URL.
- Committee CART labels may contain roles or names not represented in the
  Council roster and require resolver fixtures before indexing.
- Existing schema uniqueness on `(video_id, chunk_idx, embedding_model)` means
  a shared video must be ingested once with meeting provenance resolved first.
- A 90-day committee sweep is materially larger than the M1/M2 corpus; batch
  boundaries, retries, and manifest checkpoints are mandatory.
